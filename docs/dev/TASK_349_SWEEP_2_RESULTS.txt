THE REPAIR IS PROVEN ON ALL FOUR QUESTIONS

Q1 THE BAND MEASURES. byte reference 24 chars, graded floors alphabet=4 and
   squares=8, and both reach every later arm (E_barlink, M_dcp_draftextend,
   K_bar1_graphs all carry them). In sweep 1 every arm carried ref_text="" and
   min_score=0. The gate can now fail.
   CAVEAT, and it is my own bug, not the product's: A_default itself is FAIL.
   Its THIRD boot -- the one that regrades the baseline against the band it
   produced -- died with "CUDA out of memory ... Process 1888263 has 18.87 GiB
   memory in use". That is the SECOND baseline boot's VRAM, not yet released
   when the third started. _measure_band boots the same arm three times
   back-to-back and _terminate() returns before the child schedulers have let
   go of the cards. Named fix: wait for VRAM release (or for the child pids to
   exit) between band boots. The band itself is unaffected -- it is built from
   boots 1 and 2, both of which completed and both of whose numbers threaded.

Q2 THE REPAIRED ARMS REACH THEIR CROSSINGS, AND TWO OF THEM ARE SOUND.
   B_offload  PASS -- kv-session-offload x spec booted and stayed coherent.
   J_waveback PASS -- wave-back x PS2 likewise.
   Both crossings had NEVER been executed before this run; in sweep 1 they
   died on the KVSO_ALLOW_SPEC gate they now opt into. That is the point of
   the whole exercise: two cross-feature combinations are now known good
   rather than unknown.
   H_ps2_prefill_spill BOOTED (no error) -- the drop_flags mechanism works --
   but STOPs because its expect declares spec_algorithm=None/eagle_topk=None
   and report_effective cannot confirm a field the log does not print when
   spec is off. Matrix-side: either the reader should treat "absent" as None
   for these two fields, or the arm should not declare them.

   The remaining five hit the NEXT requirement in a chain -- each repair
   revealed the gate behind it, which is what a first pass through an
   untested arm looks like:
     C, D, G   --speculative-cross-algorithm additionally requires
               --speculative-draft-model pointing at a DFLASH checkpoint
               (the MTP rung needs no path; the DFLASH rung does).
     I         DFLASH requires --speculative-draft-model-path.
     L         "Dual-group plan is not nested -- the PD lane cannot share the
               Verband's weight bytes with this ratio pair": the 2048 MiB lane
               budget is not compatible with the TP ratio; needs tuning, not
               a flag.
     offlane   --rank-gpu-id now requires --rank-gpu-memory-mib once
               --rank-tp-ratio is not auto.
   /spinning/llm_stuff/club-3090/models-cache/qwen3.6-27b-dflash exists and is
   the checkpoint C/D/G/I need.
