2.  **Understand Tensor Parallelism:**
   - Tensor parallelism (TP) splits large weight matrices (like in linear/attention layers) across multiple GPUs.
   - Each GPU stores only a shard/partition of the weights, not the full matrix.
   - During forward/backward passes, GPUs communicate to compute partial results
