         450 cutovers matched to 100 ms samples, 28881 samples

1. THE DRAW (free at seam entry minus minimum inside the cutover), tight
   windows entry [T-1.5s,T-0.2s] / seam [T-0.2s,T+1.5s]:

     gpu0  p50=0   p90=726   p99=904   MAX=1026
     gpu1  p50=-156 p90=1184 p99=2026  MAX=2326
     gpu2  p50=104 p90=654   p99=798   MAX=1068

2. THE DRAW IS SELF-LIMITING. Conditioned on the entry level:

     gpu0 entry<1524 (n=19):  draw p50=0  MAX=456   worst in-seam min 1043
     gpu0 entry<1824 (n=252): draw p50=0  MAX=456   worst in-seam min 1043
     gpu2 entry<1824 (n=10):  draw p50=0  MAX=168   worst in-seam min 1541

   The big draws (up to 1026 MiB) only ever happen from a HIGH entry, because
   the seam's own gate frees to floor+delta+want before it stages: from a
   high entry it consumes what it had, from a low entry it consumes almost
   nothing. So the trough is NOT made by an unbounded seam appetite.

3. THE DEPTH IS INHERITED, NOT DRAWN. The 15 deepest gpu0 minima come in
   PAIRS of cutovers ~2 s apart:

       13:59:11  entry 1499  draw 456  -> min 1043
       13:59:13  entry 1043  draw   0  -> min 1043
       13:42:04  entry 1367  draw 304  -> min 1063
       13:42:05  entry 1063  draw   0  -> min 1063

   The second cutover of each pair ENTERS at the first one's trough: the seam
   does not get its memory back before the next seam begins. That is why
   s34's binding margin was +19 MiB and s36's identical trough was -23: the
   level at seam ENTRY is the whole story, and nothing was checking it.

4. WHAT A REQUIREMENT MUST BE. Because the draw from a low entry is bounded
   by ~456 MiB measured:

       required_at_entry = law(1024) + margin(256) + seam_reserve(512)
                         = 1792 MiB on the rank's own card

   A worst-case requirement (law + p90 draw of a HIGH-entry cutover) would
   arm on 77% of cutovers on gpu0 -- that is successor 36's continuous
   cache-dumper wearing a different hat, and it is rejected on his evidence.

5. RISK SET TO CLOSE: on s34's green window only 4/450 cutovers left gpu0
   below 1124 MiB and 8/450 below 1324. The mechanism has to catch a rare
   tail cheaply, not hold a high level continuously.
